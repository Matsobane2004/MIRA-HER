Privacy & safety

Plain-language answers about what we keep, what we never ask for, and how we protect you.

We never ask for

Your real name, email address, phone number, ID number, home address or photo.

What we keep

Your alias, avatar, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, and what you choose to post.

Your password and recovery code

Your password is never stored as you typed it. We keep a salted, hashed version that can’t be turned back into your password.

Because we don’t have your email, you get a one-time recovery code when you join. Keep it somewhere safe and private. It’s the only way to reset your password.

Your location

We only use your location if you choose to report a safety alert. Reports are moved by up to 150 metres so nobody can work out exactly where you were, and your alias is never shown on an alert.

Your journal and cycle tracker

Your journal is private. Only you can see it. Your cycle tracker is saved only on the device you use.

Leaving quickly and browsing safely

  • Press Esc or the Quick Exit button to leave instantly. By default you’re logged out at the same time.
  • Closing the tab logs you out, so the next person can’t open your account.
  • On a shared device, use a private or incognito window. It doesn’t keep your history.
  • Learn how to clear your browser history in your browser’s settings.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. Your posts, replies, journal, check-ins and conversations are deleted. Safety alerts you reported stay on the map to protect others, but they are no longer linked to you.

About this prototype

This hackathon version stores everything in your browser on this device, so it isn’t shared between devices. The full version will use a secure online database with encryption.
