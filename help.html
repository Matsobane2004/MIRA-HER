If you have been sexually assaulted

  • Go to the nearest Thuthuzela Care Centre, clinic or hospital as soon as you can. Thuthuzela centres offer medical care, counselling and help opening a case, all in one place.
  • Medicine to prevent HIV (PEP) works best when started within 72 hours. Emergency contraception is also available.
  • If you can, try not to wash or change clothes before you are examined, as this helps keep evidence. But please get help even if you already have.
  • It was not your fault, whatever happened.

Getting a protection order

  1. Go to your nearest magistrate’s court and ask for a protection order under the Domestic Violence Act.
  2. It’s free and you don’t need a lawyer. A court clerk will help you fill in the forms.
  3. The court can give an interim (temporary) order quickly, often on the same day.
  4. If the person breaks the order, call the police. Breaking a protection order is a crime.

Browsing safely

  • Press Esc or Quick Exit to leave this site instantly.
  • Use a private or incognito window if someone else might check your phone or computer.
  • Clear your browser history after visiting support websites if that’s safer for you.
