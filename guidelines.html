Community guidelines

These keep MIRA Her safe and supportive for everyone. Every member agrees to them when she joins.

Be kind

Everyone here is carrying something. Reply the way you’d want someone to reply to you on your hardest day.

Stay anonymous

Never share names, addresses, phone numbers, workplaces or photos that could identify you or anyone else.

No judgement

Listen to understand, not to fix or blame. Every woman’s situation and choices are her own.

Use trigger warnings

Turn on the trigger warning when your post mentions abuse, self-harm, assault or loss, so others can choose when to read it.

Share experiences, not diagnoses

Tell others what helped you, but don’t give medical or legal instructions. Point people to clinics, nurses and helplines.

No hate, harassment or scams

Racism, homophobia, xenophobia, threats, bullying, selling and scams are removed, and accounts may be closed.

What’s shared here stays here

Don’t screenshot or repost other members’ stories, even without their alias.

Report harm

Use the Report button on any post or alert that breaks these guidelines. Reports are anonymous.

How moderation works

Trained moderators review every report. They can keep a post, remove it, or close an account that keeps breaking the rules.

Posts and replies that suggest someone may be in crisis are flagged automatically. The writer immediately sees helplines and the option to talk to a listener, and a moderator is alerted to follow up. A person always makes the final decision, never the computer alone.

Peer listeners

Peer listeners are women who have been trained to listen without judgement and to recognise when someone needs professional help. They are not counsellors or doctors, and they will never ask who you are or where you live.

If you or someone else is in danger, please don’t wait for a reply in a circle. Call 10111 (112 from a cellphone) or the GBV Command Centre on 0800 428 428. More helplines
